Eddy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Eddy is a puzzle game made by Tom Glynn-Smith. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information about you. It is short because the app does very little.

What Eddy stores

Eddy keeps your scores and settings on your device only:

  • your best score for today, this week and this month, and the games behind them
  • your lifetime totals, such as games played and biggest drain
  • your settings: sound on or off, and light or dark appearance

None of this is sent anywhere. Eddy has no account system, no sign-in and no server of its own. The game itself works with no internet connection at all. If you delete the app, or reset your data from the Profile screen, that information is gone — we cannot recover it, because we never had it.

Advertising

Eddy is free and is paid for by advertising, supplied by Google AdMob. Ads are the only part of the app that connects to the internet.

To show ads, Google may collect and process:

  • a device advertising identifier
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • information about your device, such as its model and operating system version
  • how you interacted with an ad, so that advertisers are billed correctly and the same ad is not shown to you endlessly

This happens inside Google's own software, not ours. We never see it, receive it or store it. How Google uses it is described in Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choice about tracking

The first time it is relevant, Eddy asks whether you are willing to be tracked across other companies' apps and websites, using Apple's standard App Tracking Transparency prompt.

  • If you decline, ads are not personalised. You will see exactly as many ads, and the game behaves identically in every other way.
  • You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone, or turn tracking off for every app there.

Where required by law, Eddy also shows Google's consent form for advertising, and respects the choice you make in it.

Children

Eddy is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information about them. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, contact us and we will help you deal with it, though in practice there is nothing held on our side to delete.

Analytics

We run no analytics of our own. We do not know how many people play Eddy, how long for, or what scores they get. The only figures we ever see are the aggregate advertising reports Google provides, which contain no information about individuals.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to show you, correct or delete. For anything Google holds, use the links above, which include Google's own tools for access and deletion.
