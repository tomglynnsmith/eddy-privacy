How to play

  • Put each arrow on an empty square. Arrows that point into each other make a stream.
  • Run a stream into Steady Eddy and his tummy fills up — one for every tile feeding him.
  • Tap him to drain the lot and bank the points. Beat the stage number before the clock runs out.
  • A fat tummy is much better: 5 scores 250, but 20 scores 4,000.
  • A tummy of 8 or more sets off every other Steady Eddy on the board at once — a chain. Load them all up before you tap.
  • Clearing a stage is the only way to win time, and it pays 2 seconds for every point you went over.
  • Deep stages ask for more and pay less — from 10 at the start up to 20, for a fraction of the time back. However good you are, a run runs out in the end.
  • Later stages have two or three Steady Eddies, and every one of them multiplies what a drain is worth.
  • An arrow pointing at a wall, a rock, or round in a circle goes grey — stuck for good, and taking up a square.
  • Rocks are there from the start of a stage and never fall during one. A drain beside one smashes it for 100.
  • Hold puts the tile in your hand aside for later.

Your records

  • Eddy keeps your best score for today, this week and this month, and every game behind them.
  • Today starts fresh at midnight, the week every Monday, and the month on the 1st.
  • It all lives on this device and works with no connection — play on a train if you like.
